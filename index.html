WINDOWS DESKTOP APP

Make clear, useful Korean real-estate videos.

GIANT AutoStudio helps its owner create short Korean videos about housing policy and real estate using recent public sources.

How TikTok sharing works

  1. The creator signs in to their own TikTok account and grants the requested upload permission.
  2. When the creator starts an upload, AutoStudio sends the generated MP4 to that account's TikTok inbox as a draft.
  3. The creator opens the draft in TikTok, reviews or edits it, and chooses whether to publish it.

AutoStudio does not publish TikTok videos in the background. The creator controls the final post in TikTok.
